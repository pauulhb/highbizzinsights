# UAT Execution Plan

Recommended duration: 10 working days.

Users:
- 5–10 KAMs
- 2 Area/State Managers
- 1 Regional Head
- 1 Management user

Days 1–2:
Authentication, first customer creation, GPS and visit validation.

Days 3–4:
Repeat visits, samples, leads, orders and follow-ups.

Days 5–6:
Offline working, sync, duplicate prevention and app lifecycle.

Days 7–8:
Manager drill-down, reassignment, reporting and exports.

Day 9:
Regression after defect fixes.

Day 10:
Report reconciliation and sign-off decision.
