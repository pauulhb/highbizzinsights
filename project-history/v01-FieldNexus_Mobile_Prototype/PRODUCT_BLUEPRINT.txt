FIELDNEXUS – MOBILE FIELD SALES CRM
Version 0.1 Product Blueprint

Purpose
-------
A brand-neutral iOS and Android application for field-based KAMs and Sales Executives who visit Doctors, Hospitals and Distributors.

Core customer principle
-----------------------
Customer data is created by the KAM during the first physical visit.
From the second visit onward, the KAM searches and selects the existing customer and records the new interaction against the same customer master.

Core visit rule
---------------
A visit must last at least 15 minutes between verified check-in and check-out to count as a Qualified/Productive Visit.

If duration is less than 15 minutes:
- Save the visit.
- Mark it Short Visit / Not Qualified.
- Require a reason.
- Exclude it from productive-call KPI.
- Keep it visible in DWR and management review.
